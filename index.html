THE CHILOSA SPIRIT

Southwest flavor. Modern attitude.

Chilosa is a New Mexico-inspired seasoning and garnish brand built around green chile, citrus and fun. Our goal is simple: make everyday food and drinks more exciting while celebrating the flavors and culture of the Southwest.

From fruit and snacks to margaritas and cocktails, Chilosa is made to be versatile, memorable and delicious.
